Privacy Policy

Effective and last updated: October 11, 2026

GENEalogical3 is a browser-based family tree editor maintained by Jorge Hernandez. This policy explains how the app handles your information at genealogical3.vercel.app. You can use the editor without creating a GENEalogical3 account or connecting Google Drive.

Information you choose to enter

The app processes family tree names, people's names and nicknames, birth and death dates, living or deceased status, sex, relationships, notes, and portraits you enter or import. It also stores tree identifiers, edit dates, the home person, and canvas positions and zoom. This information is used to edit, display, search, and calculate relationships in your tree, and to create backups and snapshots you request. Portraits are resized in your browser.

Local storage and exports

Edited trees are saved in this browser's IndexedDB storage on your device. Family tree information and portraits are not sent to a GENEalogical3 backend. The developer cannot look up or recover your local trees. The app does not include advertising, analytics, or tracking cookies. Browser storage is used for trees and their optional Drive file links.

JSON exports contain your editable tree, notes, and portraits. PNG exports contain a picture of the tree. Downloads are created on your device; the app does not upload them. You control where you keep exports and who receives them. Consider the privacy of living relatives before recording or sharing their information.

Optional Google Drive access

Google authorization is requested only when you choose a Drive action. The app requests the https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.file permission, which limits access to files created by or authorized for this app rather than your entire Drive. GENEalogical3 lists its tree files using file identifiers, names, and modification times, reads a tree file you choose to open, and creates or updates a tree file when you choose to save. Saved files contain the tree data described above, including notes and portraits.

Transfers occur directly between your browser and Google over HTTPS. Opening a Drive tree creates a local working copy; local edits do not automatically sync to Drive. Updating an associated Drive file requires confirmation. Its identifier, name, and modification time are stored locally to associate it with your tree.

Google access tokens are held only in browser memory, are not saved to browser storage, and are not sent to a GENEalogical3 backend. The app does not request your Google password, contacts, Gmail messages, or account profile or email permissions.

GENEalogical3 follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements. Google user data is used only for the Drive features described here. It is not sold, used for advertising, transferred to data brokers, or used to train AI models. The developer does not receive or read your Drive files through the app. Files are transferred to Google only as part of the Drive actions you request.

Website hosting and third-party requests

Vercel hosts the website. Loading the site sends ordinary request information to the host, such as your IP address, browser information, requested URL, and request time. The editor loads fonts from Google Fonts, and Drive actions load Google Identity Services and use Google's APIs. These services receive network requests and may process technical information under their own policies. Font and website requests do not contain your family tree. This policy page uses system fonts and does not load Google scripts.

See the Vercel Privacy Policy and Google Privacy Policy for their handling and retention of service data, including any cookies used by Google's services.

Retention, deletion, and your choices

  • Local trees: Data remains in this browser until you delete a tree from the library, clear the site's browser data, or the browser removes its storage. Deleting a local tree also deletes its local Drive association. Clearing all site data removes all locally stored trees and associations. Back up anything you want to keep first.
  • Google Drive copies: Delete these separately in Google Drive and empty its trash to request permanent deletion under Google's retention rules. Deleting a local tree does not delete a Drive file, and deleting a Drive file does not delete local copies.
  • Google access: Choose Disconnect Drive to clear the token for the current session. Reloading or closing the page also clears it. Disconnecting does not revoke Google's permission grant or delete files. To revoke permission, remove GENEalogical3 in your Google account connections.
  • Downloads and shared copies: Delete exports from devices, backups, and services where you saved them. Recipients control copies you have shared with them.

You can inspect and correct information in the editor and export a JSON backup at any time. GENEalogical3 has no separate user account to delete. The developer does not maintain a server-side family tree database or copies of your Google access tokens.

Security

The public website and Google transfers use HTTPS. Local storage and downloaded files rely on your device and browser security; the app does not add password protection or encryption to those copies. Protect your device, Google account, and backups. No storage or transfer method can guarantee absolute security.

Changes to this policy

Updates will be published on this page with a revised date. If the app introduces a new use of Google user data, this policy and the app's disclosures will be updated, and any required consent will be requested before that use begins.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact Jorge Hernandez through the GENEalogical3 issue tracker. GitHub issues are public: do not include private family details, tree files, passwords, or access tokens. Information you voluntarily submit there is handled by GitHub under its privacy statement.
